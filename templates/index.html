About this data

Employment rates come from the Council of Ontario Universities' Common University Data Ontario (CUDO) reports and describe the graduating class of {{ results[0].grad_year if results else 2022 }}. Small programs can swing a lot from year to year.

Tuition comes from each university's 2026-27 fee information for Ontario residents. Some figures are estimates or calculations, and some include extra fees, so read the note on each card before comparing.
